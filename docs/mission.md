# Mission (approved by Daniel Spicka, October 26, 2026)

Transfer 104 N. Main Street, Sebastopol, California, from private ownership to a **community
nonprofit**, governed by a board, with theater, music, education and community gathering protected
for a defined period. During that period the board must not be able to unilaterally amend away those
protected uses. Scope includes existing cultural operators and community users, not only Wax Poetic.

Acquisition into community nonprofit ownership is the preferred outcome. Secure tenancy, a purchase
option, an aligned interim owner and phased control are **interim or fallback** strategies, never
silent replacements for the ownership objective.
